**BrowserFetch**

A powerful, all-in-one bookmarklet for Linux enthusiasts and developers. Inspired by the popular Neofetch and Screenfetch tools, BrowserFetch brings the convenience of system information and utilities right to your browser.

**Feature-Rich Toolkit**

Access a wide range of features, including:

* **Language Support**: HTML, Python, C++, and Rust, with a built-in renderer for seamless execution.
* **LLM (Large Language Model) Integration**: Harness the power of AI-driven text generation, with support for text files and more.
* **Proxy Server**: Easily switch between proxy servers for secure and private browsing.
* **Utility Suite**: Additional tools and utilities to make your browsing experience more efficient and productive.
